# Kevin black dog concept

Status: exploratory draft, not adopted in application UI.
Generated using the built-in image generation tool.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a beautifully art-directed concept presentation for Kevin, a personal AI agent, exploring an ORIGINAL BLACK DOG HEAD icon. Landscape cream canvas, spacious premium brand design. Main oversized head mark at left, lowercase wordmark "kevin" below; right side a restrained grid of four smaller applications: warm cream app tile, charcoal dark app tile, tiny flat silhouette, and attentive/thinking expression. Same recognizable character across all instances.
The dog is organic and charismatic, not a robot: a compact near-black rounded head, one upright angular ear and one softly folded ear, broad dog muzzle with subtle charcoal-on-black nose, two expressive warm ivory eyes looking alert and curious, tiny friendly asymmetric smile. Distinctive silhouette, short smooth fur suggested only through shapes, no detailed fur rendering. A small warm amber glint in the eye suggests insight and attention; the upturned folded ear and face negative space can subtly evoke a conversational mark without drawing a literal speech bubble. Personality: perceptive, calm, capable, loyal companion, slightly mischievous. The head alone must work as an app icon at 24px. Visual language: sophisticated flat vector-like forms with exceptionally subtle soft dimensional shading on the hero only, crisp graphic smaller marks, black/charcoal, ivory, one restrained amber accent. Prefer dog-specific anatomy over generic cute blob. No robot faceplate, goggles, circuits, antenna, metallic parts, collar gadgets, limbs or full body. No stock pawprint logo, no generic AI sparkle floating above head, no resemblance to existing branded dog mascots. Beautiful balance of originality, simplicity and warmth. Only text: "kevin" and small heading "BLACK DOG / CONCEPT 02". No extra explanatory text, no watermarks. This is one cohesive concept board, not a website screenshot.

## Review
