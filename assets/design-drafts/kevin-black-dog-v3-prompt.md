## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand, style-transfer refinement.
Refine the attached Kevin black dog brand concept into a radically simpler, distinctive, premium SOFTWARE AGENT ICON identity. Preserve black dog identity, one upright ear and one folded ear, warm amber accent, approachable intelligent companion personality. REPLACE the rendered animation-movie puppy aesthetic entirely with extremely clean FLAT geometric vector-like branding. No 3D, shadows, gradients, fur, anatomical nostrils, big glossy puppy eyes, cheeks, eyebrows or paws. This is a designed symbol, not an animal illustration.
Design one consistent original front-facing compact head silhouette: softly squared charcoal dog face flowing into an angular upright ear on left and a clearly folded ear on right. Minimal broad dog muzzle indicated with just one tiny rounded triangular nose and one short mouth stroke. Eyes are two warm ivory short rounded horizontal shapes with small charcoal gaze notches; one has a tiny amber cursor-like accent integrated into the eye. The eyes should feel attentive and intentional, with a subtle asymmetry. NO face screen, NO robot mask, NO antennas, NO circuitry, NO floating AI sparkles. Dog must remain immediately identifiable. Make ear geometry and a little negative-space cut at the jaw give a subtle conversational character. All geometry bold and readable as a small app icon. Character should feel clever and gently mischievous, not infantile.
Presentation: polished spacious landscape identity board on warm ivory. Left half: one very large flat head icon with lowercase black 'kevin' wordmark underneath in friendly modern sans serif. Right upper: two smaller app-icon tiles in ivory and deep charcoal; dark version uses a subtle warm-gray head silhouette and ivory/amber eyes for real contrast. Right lower: three small HEAD ONLY versions of the exact same silhouette, expressing Ready, Listening, Thinking through eye marks only. Labels only 'READY', 'LISTENING', 'THINKING'. Small title 'KEVIN / CONCEPT 03'. Large clean margins, precise layout. The main hero and ALL small versions must use identical FLAT visual language and near-identical geometry, not a mix of 3D and flat. No extra UI, no decorative elements. Aim for a memorable open-source AI agent brand mark suitable for a menu bar, avatar, favicon and repository logo.

## Review

Simplified silhouette and cursor eye communicate software interaction more clearly. The cursor is still literal and could be integrated more naturally. Ready and Thinking states are too similar; these are concept samples, not validated state assets. Small-size readability remains untested.
