# Kevin App conversation concept

Static UI concept generated with built-in imagegen. Reference: kevin-doodle-antenna-v1.png. Shows home and conversation states; not an implemented app screenshot. Existing promotional and interactive prototypes preserved.

## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Draw a high-fidelity desktop APPLICATION interaction prototype for Kevin, a personal AI companion, using the attached antenna dog as exact mascot identity reference. This is an actual daily-use conversation app, NOT a marketing website, landing page, brand guidelines, or document dashboard.
Output one wide landscape design image with TWO complete app windows side-by-side. Left window about 38% width, right window 62%. Small external labels above windows in Chinese: "01 / 等你开口" and "02 / 陪你工作". Show all UI including bottom input bars, no cropping. Restrained contemporary product craft, warm ivory paper #FAF7F0, near-black ink #292621, fine warm gray lines, amber #DEA74E accents. Only the mascot and wordmark are hand-drawn; interface typography is clean extremely legible Chinese sans-serif. Generous but functional spacing, no oversized advertising headlines, no gradients, no cartoon UI controls. Very subtle paper grain. Dog: same broad round face, black side patches, short floppy ears, horizontal normal dot eyes, small amber cheek, curved antenna with amber dot. NEVER letter K in the eyes, NEVER rabbit ears.

LEFT: app home, minimal narrow top toolbar with small history icon left, handwritten "kevin" wordmark, plus icon right. No permanent sidebar. Near middle a medium friendly antenna dog head with one short ear gently lifted, greeting below "今天想做什么？". Directly below a wide rounded input area reading "交给我一件事…" with paperclip and a small amber circular send button. Bottom of input has tiny hint "随时补充，我在听". Under this, a small section "接着上次" with two modest thin paper slips "整理项目说明" and "周末旅行计划" and small timestamps. Lots of restful breathing room without looking like a marketing hero. Bottom small settings icon and subtle "本地工作区" label.

RIGHT: same app after a conversation has started. Top toolbar history icon, small kevin wordmark, centered conversation title "整理项目说明", top-right overflow icon. No sidebar and no large mascot repeating in answers. Single flowing conversation laid out like collaborative notes:
At top a modest pale sand user note aligned toward right: "帮我整理 README，让新人更容易上手。"
Below, Kevin response left aligned directly on the paper, no enclosing bubble and no repeated robot avatar: "我先看一下现有说明，把安装步骤和能力边界梳理清楚。"
Then a compact expandable progress line with small amber dot and chevron: "已读 3 个文件 · 草稿已整理". Show one thin vertical ink guide tying this response, progress and result together into a single task segment.
Next a compact result slip, NOT a giant full document: small document icon, title "README 改版草稿", subtitle "重排结构 · 补充启动说明", a restrained "打开查看 ↗" button.
Below that a warm amber-tinted inline approval note: "准备更新 README.md", explanation "修改 1 个文件，先确认这一版。", clear buttons "查看差异", "允许本次" and a quieter "暂不修改". Keep this region clean and easy to scan.
BOTTOM of right app: fixed composer dock with a SMALL dog head attached just above/at left of its border, as a companion resting at the input edge. It has a short half-raised ear and a gentle curious head tilt. A small status near the dog says "等你确认". Wide multiline input reading "继续补充，或告诉我哪里要改…" with paperclip and amber send arrow. Underneath concise understated tool row "工作区 · 模型" and "暂停" control. Dog is located at input once, not as an avatar beside every response. The visual point is the dog moving from center of home to being a persistent companion at the input dock.
Between windows in outer margin, a very small hand-drawn curved arrow can suggest the dog’s transition, but do not clutter screens with explanatory annotations.
Make the interaction feel personal, original, calm, and usable over long conversations. Prioritize readable Chinese text, complete app layouts, beautiful typography and clarity. No huge side panels, no three-column productivity dashboard, no feature card grid, no fake browser chrome or code.
