Preview concept sheet generated using built-in imagegen. Reference: kevin-doodle-v4.png. Not integrated into UI.

## Prompt

Create a six-state expression sheet based faithfully on the attached Kevin dog doodle. Preserve this EXACT character identity and drawing style: broad rounded cream face, black patches down the sides, short floppy black ears with the viewer-right ear folded outward, big rounded triangular black nose, simple dot eyes on the same horizontal line, a tiny muted amber dot on viewer-right cheek, thick imperfect crayon/ink strokes on warm cream paper. No redesign, no 3D, no glossy eyes, no rabbit ears, no robot features or cursor in eyes. Each head front-facing, same scale, same proportions and ear silhouette across all six states. No bodies or hands.
Layout: a beautiful spacious 3-column by 2-row grid, six clearly separated equally sized character heads, generous margins. Small handwritten heading "kevin" at top. Each head has exactly one legible short Chinese label beneath, in this order:
Top left "待命": relaxed dot eyes, original soft double-curve smile, no symbol.
Top middle "倾听": attentive round dot eyes still level, small gentle smile, two tiny hand-drawn curved listening marks beside one ear.
Top right "思考": two short horizontal eye strokes at the same height, tiny pursed curved mouth, a small hand-drawn thought bubble with three dots above the head.
Bottom left "执行": focused short horizontal eyes, slight determined but friendly smile, two small amber motion strokes beside the head. Must not look angry, no furrowed brows.
Bottom middle "完成": two joyful closed upward-curved eyes, broad happy open smile, one small hand-drawn amber check mark near the upper right of the head.
Bottom right "待确认": round level dot eyes, a small round open mouth, a small amber question mark beside the head, curious and polite, not sad or distressed.
The facial expressions must be clearly distinct even without labels; Thinking and Executing must differ in mouth and supporting marks. Keep restrained black ink and muted amber accent only, cream background, charming economical pen drawing, no cards, borders, shadows, extra UI, or extra words. This is a cohesive character expression study for a personal agent interface, not final production assets.
